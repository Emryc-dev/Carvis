# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Car enthusiasts using the Android application in real-world capture conditions to identify vehicles, inspect trustworthy results, and collect recognized cars in a personal Garage.

## Product Purpose

CarVision turns a camera capture or imported vehicle photo into a real, backend-verified vehicle discovery. The scan experience must keep the selected vehicle visually present, explain genuine processing stages, present authoritative rarity and XP data, and let the user explicitly decide whether to add the discovery to the Garage.

## Brand Personality

Premium, automotive, controlled. Technical enough to feel capable, but calm and legible rather than futuristic or theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic Android loading screens, fake HUD telemetry, excessive neon or gradients, giant spinners, mock vehicle data, duplicated scanner experiences, and automatic navigation that removes the user from the active scan flow.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the captured or imported vehicle as the visual anchor throughout analysis.
2. Show only real application state and server-authoritative data.
3. Make discovery and collection feel like one continuous, intentional workflow.
4. Preserve user control over retrying, leaving, collecting, or scanning again.
5. Reuse the existing product language so discoveries and Garage collectibles feel identical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every key action needs a meaningful content description, state cannot rely on color alone, text must remain readable over imagery, touch targets must remain comfortable, and motion must be short, purposeful, and non-blocking.
